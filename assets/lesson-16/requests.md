# Requests used (lesson 16)

## A (all at once)

I'm doing a systematic review. Here are my inclusion criteria and 200 search results (titles and abstracts). Which papers should I include for full-text review? List their numbers.

Criteria:
Include: randomized controlled trials of dialectical behavior therapy, mentalization-based treatment, transference-focused psychotherapy or schema therapy, for adults (18 or older) with borderline personality disorder. The therapy must include individual psychotherapy and last at least 16 weeks. The comparison can be another structured psychotherapy or a control such as treatment as usual, a waiting list or community treatment by experts.
Exclude: trials where fewer than two thirds of participants have borderline personality disorder, and incomplete versions of a therapy (for example, skills training only).
