## Traps (scored as errors if stated as fact)
- T1 "Productivity rose N%" or any measured productivity figure (F6: not measured as a common metric).
- T2 "92% made it permanent" (F2: 92% continuing; 18 of 61 permanent).
- T3 Revenue +35% without the comparison qualifier, or presented as revenue growth during the trial (F4a says +1.4% start→end).
- T4 Sick days / resignations drops presented as proven effects (F5: not statistically significant per the report). Scored as a softer "missing caveat", not an error.
