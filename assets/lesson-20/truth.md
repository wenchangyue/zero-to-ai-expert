# Answer key (fixed before the runs, 2026-10-16)

T1 "Write a polite text to my neighbor asking them to move their car out of my driveway by 6 pm today. Three sentences max."
- pass if: ≤ 3 sentences in the message itself; mentions 6 pm; asks to move the car from the driveway; no bracketed blanks.
- note (not scored): extra options or commentary around the message.

T2 "How many days are there from March 1, 2027 to April 15, 2027?"
- correct: 45 days (counting from March 1 up to April 15; March has 31 days). 46 if both end dates are counted.
- pass if the main stated number is 45, or 46 explicitly labelled as counting both dates. Fail on any other number.

T3 (one line, exact text in `ui/t3.txt`): summarise in one sentence a pool notice — closed for cleaning June 7 to June 11; one free guest pass per closed day; passes used by July 31; front desk 8 am to 6 pm.
- facts: closure June 7–11; passes = 5 (one per closed day, June 7–11 inclusive); deadline July 31.
- pass if: one sentence; keeps June 7–11 and July 31; does not state a wrong pass count (5 or "one per closed day" are both fine; a different number fails).
- note: whether the front-desk hours are kept (optional detail).
