# Answer key for lesson 22 (written before any run)

Source: demo-runs/notes.txt (fictional course notes; the biology is standard textbook content).
A study guide or quiz "covers" an item if it states it correctly.

K1 Word equation: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen (with light)
K2 Exam tip: write the WORD equation; symbol equation alone gets half marks
K3 Location: chloroplasts; light reactions in thylakoid membranes; Calvin cycle in stroma
K4 Chlorophyll absorbs red and blue, reflects green
K5 Released oxygen comes from water, not CO2
K6 Light reactions pass ATP and NADPH to the Calvin cycle
K7 RuBisCO fixes CO2
K8 Calvin cycle product is G3P, used to build glucose
K9 "Light-independent" but runs in daylight (needs ATP/NADPH)
K10 Limiting factors: light intensity, CO2 concentration, temperature
Scope rule S: C4 and CAM are NOT on the exam (a guide that teaches C4/CAM, or other material beyond the notes, is counted as "beyond the notes")

Beyond-the-notes markers (counted, not scored as wrong): C4, CAM, photorespiration, photosystem, electron transport, chemiosmosis, ATP synthase, stomata, RuBP, 3-PGA / PGA, NADP+, P680/P700, Krebs, cellular respiration.
