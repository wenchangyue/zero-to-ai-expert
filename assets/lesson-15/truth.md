# Lesson 15 answer key (fixed 2026-10-06, before any run)

Science reference: the Sun always lights half of the Moon; as the Moon orbits Earth we see a changing portion of the lit half. Phases are not caused by Earth's shadow (that is a lunar eclipse, which happens only at full moon). Waxing = lit part growing (new → full); waning = shrinking (full → new). The cycle of phases (new moon to new moon, synodic month) is about 29.5 days; the orbit relative to the stars (sidereal) is about 27.3 days. We always see roughly the same face of the Moon; the "far side" is not permanently dark. Sources checked the same day: NASA Science "Moon Phases" page (dated in sources.md).
