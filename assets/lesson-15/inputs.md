# Lesson 15 inputs (fictional, written 2026-10-06 for this lesson)

Syllabus line (Grade 7 science): Students explain why the Moon appears to change shape over a month.

Exit-ticket question: Why does the Moon have phases?

Student A: The Sun always lights half of the Moon. As the Moon goes around Earth, we see different amounts of that lit half. At full moon we see all of the lit side, and at new moon we see none of it.

Student B: The Moon has phases because Earth's shadow covers part of it. When the shadow covers all of it, that's a new moon. As the shadow moves away, the Moon gets bigger until it's full.

Student C: Half of the Moon is always lit by the Sun, and we see more or less of the lit half as the Moon orbits us, which takes about a month. When the Moon is waxing it gets smaller, and when it is waning it gets bigger.
