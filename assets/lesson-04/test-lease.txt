1. PARTIES AND PREMISES

1.1 This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Alder Row Property Management ("Landlord") and the person or persons signing below as tenant ("Tenant").

1.2 Landlord agrees to rent to Tenant, and Tenant agrees to rent from Landlord, the apartment identified as Unit 4B in the building known as Alder Row Apartments, together with the appliances and fixtures listed in the Move-In Condition Report (the "Premises").

1.3 The Premises shall be used only as a private residence. No business that brings clients, customers or deliveries of goods for resale to the Premises may be operated from the Premises. Working from home on a computer or telephone is not a business use under this section.

1.4 Only the persons listed on the application and approved by Landlord may live in the Premises. A guest who stays more than fourteen (14) nights in any six-month period becomes an occupant and must apply to be added to this Lease.

1.5 Tenant acknowledges that Tenant has inspected the Premises, or has had the opportunity to inspect the Premises, and accepts them in their present condition except as noted in the Move-In Condition Report.

2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 "Common Areas" means hallways, stairways, the lobby, the mail room, the laundry room, the courtyard, the bicycle room and any other area of the property that is not part of a rented unit.

2.2 "Rules" means the Community Rules in Sections 14 through 17 of this Lease and any written rules Landlord adopts later under Section 17.

2.3 "Business Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays.

2.4 "Written notice" means notice delivered in the manner described in Section 20.

3. TERM AND RENT

3.1 The term of this Lease begins on the Commencement Date shown on the signature page and ends at 11:59 p.m. on the day before the first anniversary of the Commencement Date (the "Initial Term"), unless ended earlier or extended under this Lease.

3.2 Tenant shall pay monthly rent of one thousand six hundred fifty dollars ($1,650.00) ("Monthly Rent").

3.3 Monthly Rent is due in advance on the first (1st) day of each calendar month without demand. If the Commencement Date is not the first day of a month, rent for the first partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis and paid before Tenant receives keys.

3.4 Rent may be paid through the resident portal, by personal check, by cashier's check or by money order. Cash is not accepted at the management office. Landlord may require certified funds after any payment is returned unpaid.

3.5 Payments are applied first to unpaid fees and charges, then to past-due rent, then to current rent, regardless of any note written on the payment.

3.6 Rent is not reduced because Tenant is absent from the Premises, does not use an amenity, or is temporarily unable to use part of the Premises during a repair, except as required by law.

3.7 A returned payment fee of thirty-five dollars ($35.00) is charged for each payment returned unpaid by a bank for any reason.

3.8 Late Charges. If Landlord has not received the full Monthly Rent by 11:59 p.m. on the fifth (5th) day of the month, Tenant shall pay an initial late charge of seventy-five dollars ($75.00), plus ten dollars ($10.00) for each additional day the rent remains unpaid, up to a maximum late charge of one hundred seventy-five dollars ($175.00) for that month.

3.9 Landlord's acceptance of a late charge does not waive Tenant's obligation to pay rent on time and does not waive Landlord's right to end this Lease for nonpayment.

3.10 Late charges are a reasonable estimate of Landlord's administrative costs and lost interest caused by late payment. They are not a penalty.

3.11 If rent is late in three (3) or more months during any twelve-month period, Landlord may require that all future rent be paid by cashier's check or money order.

3.12 Tenant may request a rent receipt for any payment. Receipts for portal payments are available in the resident portal.

3.13 Tenant shall not withhold rent for any reason, including a pending repair request, except where a statute expressly permits withholding and Tenant has followed every step that statute requires.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

4.1 On or before signing this Lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of one thousand six hundred fifty dollars ($1,650.00) (the "Security Deposit").

4.2 The Security Deposit secures Tenant's performance under this Lease. It is not a limit on Tenant's liability and may not be used by Tenant as payment of the last month's rent.

4.3 Landlord will return the Security Deposit, less lawful deductions, together with an itemized list of any deductions, within thirty (30) days after Tenant surrenders the Premises and returns all keys.

4.4 Lawful deductions include unpaid rent, unpaid fees, the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear, the cost of removing property or trash left in the Premises, and any other charge this Lease states may be deducted from the Security Deposit.

4.5 Tenant must give Landlord a forwarding address in writing. If Tenant does not, Landlord will send the deposit and itemized list to the Premises address.

4.6 The Security Deposit does not earn interest for Tenant unless the law requires it.

4.7 If Landlord sells the property, Landlord may transfer the Security Deposit to the buyer, and Tenant will then look only to the buyer for its return.

5. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

5.1 Landlord pays for water, sewer and trash collection. Tenant pays for electricity, gas, internet, television and telephone service to the Premises.

5.2 Tenant shall open accounts in Tenant's own name for electricity and gas effective on the Commencement Date and keep them active until Tenant surrenders the Premises. If service lapses and Landlord pays to restore it, Tenant shall repay Landlord within ten (10) days of a written request.

5.3 Tenant shall keep the Premises heated to at least fifty-five degrees Fahrenheit (55°F) at all times from October 1 through April 30 to prevent frozen pipes, including while Tenant is away.

5.4 Landlord is not responsible for interruptions in utilities or services caused by the utility provider, by weather, or by repairs, as long as Landlord makes reasonable efforts to restore service.

5.5 Tenant shall not tamper with meters, connect to another unit's service, or run cords or hoses from the Premises into Common Areas.

6. APPLIANCES

6.1 The Premises include a refrigerator, a range, a dishwasher and a microwave. Landlord will repair or replace these appliances if they stop working through normal use.

6.2 Tenant shall not install a washer, a dryer, a portable dishwasher, a window air conditioner or a freezer without Landlord's written consent.

7. USE AND CARE OF THE PREMISES

7.1 Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and sanitary and shall use all fixtures and appliances in a reasonable manner.

7.2 Tenant shall promptly report water leaks, mold, broken windows, loss of heat and any condition that could cause damage or injury.

7.3 Tenant is responsible for the cost of repairing any damage caused by Tenant, occupants, guests or pets, other than normal wear and tear.

7.4 Tenant shall replace light bulbs, smoke-detector batteries and HVAC filters at Tenant's own expense. Replacement filters of the correct size are available at the management office.

7.5 Tenant shall not flush anything other than toilet paper and human waste. The cost of clearing a drain blocked by grease, wipes, hygiene products or similar items will be charged to Tenant.

7.6 Tenant shall not paint, wallpaper, install shelving, change locks or make any other alteration to the Premises without Landlord's written consent. Small nails for hanging pictures are permitted.

7.7 Any alteration made without consent may be removed by Landlord, and the cost of restoring the Premises will be charged to Tenant.

7.8 Tenant shall not keep flammable liquids, other than ordinary household products, in the Premises or in any storage area.

8. EARLY TERMINATION BY TENANT

8.1 Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the Initial Term only by (a) giving Landlord written notice at least sixty (60) days before the date Tenant will move out, and (b) paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' Monthly Rent.

8.2 The early termination fee is due with the notice. Rent remains due through the move-out date stated in the notice.

8.3 Paying the early termination fee does not release Tenant from charges for damage to the Premises or from any other amount owed under this Lease.

8.4 If Tenant moves out without following Section 8.1, Tenant remains liable for rent through the end of the Initial Term, less any rent Landlord receives from a replacement tenant, plus Landlord's reasonable costs of re-renting.

8.5 Nothing in this Section limits any right Tenant has under law to end this Lease early because of military deployment, domestic violence or another reason a statute recognizes. Tenant should provide the documents that statute requires.

9. ENTRY BY LANDLORD

9.1 Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times to make repairs, inspect, show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, or provide agreed services. Landlord will give at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice except in an emergency.

9.2 In an emergency, including fire, flooding, a gas smell or a report that a person inside may need help, Landlord may enter without notice and will leave a written note stating the time and reason for entry.

9.3 During the last thirty (30) days of the Lease, Landlord may show the Premises to prospective tenants on twenty-four (24) hours' notice between 9:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.

9.4 Tenant shall not add or change a lock or install a device that prevents Landlord's key from working.

10. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE REQUESTS

10.1 Non-emergency repair requests must be submitted through the resident portal or in writing at the management office. Landlord will respond to non-emergency requests within five (5) Business Days.

10.2 Emergencies, including no heat in winter, a major water leak, sewage backup, a gas smell or a lock that does not secure the front door, should be reported by telephone to the emergency maintenance line at any hour.

10.3 Submitting a repair request authorizes Landlord to enter the Premises to make that repair without further notice during normal business hours.

10.4 Landlord will not be liable for delay in repairs caused by the unavailability of parts or contractors, provided Landlord keeps Tenant informed.

10.5 If a repair was made necessary by Tenant's misuse or neglect, Landlord will charge Tenant the reasonable cost of the repair.

11. INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

11.1 Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's personal property. Tenant is strongly encouraged to carry renter's insurance with personal liability coverage.

11.2 Landlord is not liable for loss of or damage to Tenant's property caused by theft, fire, water, weather or other causes, unless caused by Landlord's negligence.

11.3 Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against claims arising from the negligence of Tenant, occupants or guests in the Premises or the Common Areas.

11.4 Tenant shall notify Landlord promptly of any injury that occurs in the Premises or the Common Areas.

12. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

12.1 Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent.

12.2 A request to sublet must be made in writing at least thirty (30) days before the proposed start of the sublease, must identify the proposed subtenant, and must be accompanied by a nonrefundable sublease administration fee of one hundred dollars ($100.00).

12.3 The proposed subtenant must complete Landlord's standard application and meet Landlord's rental criteria.

12.4 Tenant remains fully responsible under this Lease during any sublease.

12.5 Short-term rentals of any length through any online platform are not permitted and are treated as a material breach of this Lease.

13. PETS

13.1 No animal may be kept in the Premises, even temporarily, except as allowed in this Section or required by law for an assistance animal.

13.2 Dogs are not permitted.

13.3 Tenant may keep up to two (2) cats after signing Landlord's Pet Addendum. Tenant shall pay a nonrefundable pet fee of three hundred dollars ($300.00) per household and monthly pet rent of forty dollars ($40.00) per cat, added to Monthly Rent.

13.4 Fish in a tank of twenty (20) gallons or less and caged birds are permitted without a pet fee.

13.5 Tenant is responsible for any damage, odor or pest problem caused by an animal, and for keeping the animal from disturbing other residents.

13.6 An unauthorized animal found in the Premises will result in a charge of fifty dollars ($50.00) per day until it is removed or a Pet Addendum is signed.

13.7 A request for an assistance animal will be handled under Landlord's reasonable accommodation policy and is not subject to the pet fee, pet rent or the limits in this Section.

COMMUNITY RULES

14. NOISE AND CONDUCT

14.1 Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. Sunday through Thursday and 11:00 p.m. to 8:00 a.m. on Friday and Saturday nights.

14.2 At all times, Tenant, occupants and guests shall not make noise that can be heard in another unit at a level that interferes with a neighbor's reasonable use of that unit.

14.3 Tenant is responsible for the conduct of occupants and guests in the Premises and in the Common Areas.

14.4 Harassment of other residents, staff or contractors is a material breach of this Lease.

14.5 Moving furniture into or out of the building is permitted only between 8:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

15. SMOKING

15.1 Smoking of any substance, including the use of electronic cigarettes and vaporizers, is prohibited inside the Premises, on balconies and in all Common Areas.

15.2 Smoking is permitted only in the designated area at the north end of the courtyard, at least twenty-five (25) feet from any door or window.

15.3 The cost of cleaning, painting or replacing items to remove smoke odor or residue will be charged to Tenant.

16. COMMON AREAS AND BUILDING SERVICES

16.1 Laundry Room. The laundry room is open from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. daily. Machines are operated by card or mobile payment. Items left in machines for more than one hour may be removed and placed on the folding table.

16.2 Packages. Packages delivered to the mail room are placed in the package lockers when space allows. Packages not collected within seven (7) days may be returned to the carrier.

16.3 Bicycles. Bicycles may be stored in the bicycle room or inside the Premises. Bicycles may not be stored in hallways, stairways or on balconies where visible from the street.

16.4 Balconies. Balconies must be kept free of trash, stored furniture other than outdoor seating, and items hung over the railing. Charcoal and propane grills are not permitted anywhere on the property.

16.5 Trash. Trash must be bagged and placed in the dumpsters in the courtyard enclosure. Recycling must be placed in the marked bins. Large items must be arranged through the management office.

16.6 Hallways. Doormats are permitted. Shoes, strollers and other items may not be left in hallways.

16.7 Keys and Fobs. Each Tenant receives two unit keys, one mailbox key and one building fob per adult occupant. Replacement fobs cost twenty-five dollars ($25.00) each.

16.8 Pest Control. Landlord will treat the Premises for insects or rodents at no charge during the first thirty (30) days after the Commencement Date. After that period, Tenant shall pay ninety-five dollars ($95.00) for each treatment of the Premises, unless the same pest is found in two or more neighboring units at the same time, in which case Landlord pays.

16.9 Tenant shall report signs of pests promptly and shall prepare the Premises for treatment as instructed. A treatment that cannot be completed because the Premises were not prepared will still be charged.

16.10 Tenant shall not bring used mattresses, upholstered furniture or bedding into the building without first inspecting them for bed bugs.

17. CHANGES TO THE RULES

17.1 Landlord may adopt reasonable new written rules for the safety, care and cleanliness of the property and the comfort of residents.

17.2 A new rule takes effect thirty (30) days after written notice to Tenant, applies to all residents equally, and may not change the Monthly Rent, the Security Deposit or the Term.

18. DEFAULT

18.1 Tenant is in default if Tenant fails to pay rent or any other amount when due, fails to comply with any other term of this Lease after written notice and a reasonable time to cure, or gives false information on the rental application.

--- Page 14 of 20 ---

18.2 If Tenant is in default, Landlord may end this Lease by written notice as permitted by law and may recover possession of the Premises through the legal process required by law. Landlord will not change the locks, remove doors or shut off utilities to force Tenant out.

18.3 If Landlord brings a legal action to enforce this Lease and wins, Tenant shall pay Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees and court costs to the extent allowed by law.

18.4 Landlord's failure to enforce any term on one occasion does not waive Landlord's right to enforce it later.

19. ABANDONMENT

19.1 The Premises are considered abandoned if rent is unpaid for at least fifteen (15) days, Tenant appears to have moved out, and Tenant does not respond within five (5) days to a written notice posted on the door and sent to Tenant's email address on file.

19.2 Property left in abandoned Premises will be stored and disposed of as the law requires. Tenant is responsible for reasonable costs of removal and storage.

19.3 Abandonment does not release Tenant from the obligation to pay rent through the end of the Term, less any rent Landlord receives from a replacement tenant.

20. NOTICES

20.1 Written notice to Landlord must be delivered by hand to the management office, sent by certified mail to the management office address on the signature page, or submitted through the notices function of the resident portal.

20.2 Written notice to Tenant may be delivered by hand, left in a sealed envelope at the door of the Premises, sent by first-class mail to the Premises, or sent to Tenant's email address on file.

20.3 A notice sent by mail is effective three (3) days after mailing. A notice delivered by hand, left at the door, submitted through the portal or sent by email is effective on the day it is delivered or sent.

20.4 Text messages and verbal statements are not written notice under this Lease.

21. MOVE-IN AND MOVE-OUT

21.1 Before receiving keys, Tenant and Landlord will complete and sign a Move-In Condition Report describing the condition of the Premises. Tenant may add notes to the report for seven (7) days after move-in.

21.2 Tenant may request a pre-move-out inspection during the last fourteen (14) days of the Lease to learn what Landlord expects to deduct from the Security Deposit.

21.3 At move-out, Tenant shall leave the Premises clean, remove all personal property and trash, and return all keys, fobs and parking or access cards.

21.4 Carpet Cleaning. Because carpets must be professionally cleaned between residents, a carpet cleaning charge of one hundred fifty dollars ($150.00) will be deducted from the Security Deposit at move-out regardless of the condition of the carpet.

21.5 Keys not returned will be charged at the cost of rekeying the Premises.

21.6 If Tenant leaves property behind after move-out, Landlord may store or dispose of it as the law allows, and the cost will be deducted from the Security Deposit.

21.7 If Tenant stays in the Premises after the Lease ends without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall pay rent for each day of holdover at one and one-half (1.5) times the daily rate of the Monthly Rent, and is responsible for any losses Landlord suffers because the Premises were not available to a new tenant.

22. RELOCATION AND CASUALTY

22.1 If the Premises become uninhabitable because of fire, flood or another casualty not caused by Tenant, either party may end this Lease by written notice, and rent will be prorated to the date of the casualty.

22.2 If only part of the Premises is unusable, rent will be reduced in proportion to the part that cannot be used until repairs are complete.

22.3 If the casualty was caused by Tenant, occupants, guests or pets, rent does not abate and Tenant is responsible for the cost of repair to the extent not covered by Landlord's insurance.

23. SMOKE AND CARBON MONOXIDE ALARMS

23.1 Landlord has installed working smoke alarms and a carbon monoxide alarm in the Premises. Tenant shall test the alarms monthly and replace batteries as needed.

23.2 Tenant shall not disconnect, remove or cover any alarm. Tenant shall report a malfunctioning alarm immediately.

23.3 Tenant shall pay for any fire department charge or damage resulting from Tenant disabling an alarm.

24. LEAD-BASED PAINT AND OTHER DISCLOSURES

24.1 The building was constructed after 1978. No lead-based paint disclosure is required.

24.2 Tenant acknowledges receipt of any other disclosures required by law, which are attached to this Lease as addenda.

25. RENEWAL

25.1 Automatic Renewal. At the end of the Initial Term, this Lease will automatically renew for an additional term of twelve (12) months unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least seventy-five (75) days before the last day of the Initial Term.

25.2 Landlord may increase the Monthly Rent for a renewal term by up to six percent (6%) by giving Tenant written notice of the new rent at least ninety (90) days before the last day of the current term.

25.3 All other terms of this Lease continue to apply during a renewal term, and each renewal term will itself renew under Section 25.1 unless notice of non-renewal is given.

25.4 A notice of non-renewal does not need to state a reason.

26. MISCELLANEOUS

26.1 Entire Agreement. This Lease, together with its addenda and the Move-In Condition Report, is the entire agreement between the parties. No verbal promise is binding unless written into this Lease or an addendum signed by both parties.

26.2 Severability. If any part of this Lease is found to be invalid, the rest of this Lease remains in effect.

26.4 Joint and Several Liability. If more than one person signs this Lease as Tenant, each person is responsible for the full amount of rent and all other obligations, not just a share.

26.5 Governing Law. This Lease is governed by the laws of the state in which the Premises are located.

26.6 Time. Time is of the essence for all payment and notice deadlines in this Lease.

26.7 Headings. Section headings are for convenience only and do not change the meaning of any section.

26.8 Counterparts and Electronic Signatures. This Lease may be signed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is an original.

26.9 Changes. This Lease may be changed only by a written amendment signed by Landlord and all Tenants.

26.10 Waiver of Jury Trial. To the extent permitted by law, the parties waive trial by jury in any action arising from this Lease.
