Hollow Oak Bakery: custom cake policy (fictional, written for this lesson)

- Ordering: custom cakes need at least 5 days' notice. Orders with less notice: the owner decides case by case; staff must not promise them.
- Prices: 6-inch cake (serves 8 to 10) from $45. 8-inch cake (serves 14 to 16) from $65. Final price depends on the design.
- Deposit: 50% deposit confirms the order.
- Cancellation: deposit refunded in full if you cancel at least 72 hours before pickup. No refund after that.
- Pickup and delivery: pickup is free. Delivery within 10 miles costs $15. No deliveries on Sundays.
- Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 7 am to 4 pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.
- Allergens: our kitchen uses nuts, wheat, dairy and eggs. We cannot guarantee any cake is free of these allergens. We do not make gluten-free cakes.
- Problems with an order: contact us within 24 hours with a photo. The owner reviews every case and decides what we can offer; staff must not promise refunds or replacements.
