RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE FOR A LESSON)

This Residential Lease Agreement is made between Maple Court Properties ("Landlord") and the person named as Tenant on the signature page ("Tenant") for the apartment at Maple Court, Unit 3B ("the Premises"). Every name, address and number in this document is invented.

1. TERM
1.1 The lease begins on November 1, 2026 and ends on October 31, 2027 ("the Term").
1.2 Tenant may take possession of the Premises on the start date once the first month's rent and the security deposit have been received.
1.3 If Tenant stays in the Premises after the end of the Term with Landlord's consent, the tenancy continues month to month on the same terms, except that either party may end it with 30 days' written notice.

2. RENT
2.1 Monthly rent is $1,850, due on the first day of each month.
2.2 Rent may be paid by bank transfer or through the resident portal. Cash is not accepted.
2.3 If rent is not received by the end of the fifth day of the month, Tenant will owe a late fee of $75. The late fee is charged once per month and does not increase.
2.4 A payment returned by the bank for any reason will be treated as unpaid and will also incur a $35 returned-payment charge.
2.5 Landlord will not raise the rent during the Term.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Tenant will pay a security deposit of $1,850 before move-in.
3.2 The deposit may be used to cover unpaid rent, cleaning beyond normal wear and tear, and damage caused by Tenant, household members or guests.
3.3 Landlord will return the deposit, minus any lawful deductions and with an itemized list of those deductions, within 30 days after Tenant moves out and returns all keys.
3.4 The deposit may not be used by Tenant as the last month's rent.

4. UTILITIES AND SERVICES
4.1 Landlord pays for water, sewer and trash collection.
4.2 Tenant pays for electricity, gas and internet, and must set up these accounts in Tenant's own name before the start date.
4.3 Landlord is not responsible for interruptions in service caused by utility providers, but will report building-wide outages to the provider promptly.

5. USE OF THE PREMISES
5.1 The Premises may be used only as a private residence for Tenant and the household members listed on the application.
5.2 Guests may stay for no more than 14 nights in any 6-month period without Landlord's written consent.
5.3 Tenant may not run a business that brings customers or deliveries of goods for sale to the Premises.
5.4 Quiet hours are from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. every day. During quiet hours, noise must not be audible in neighboring units.
5.5 Smoking of any kind, including vaping, is not allowed anywhere in the Premises, on balconies, or within 25 feet of any building entrance.

6. PETS
6.1 Tenant may keep one cat or one dog weighing under 25 pounds, after giving Landlord the animal's name, breed and vaccination record.
6.2 Tenant will pay a one-time, refundable pet deposit of $300 before the animal moves in. The pet deposit is returned under the same rules as the security deposit.
6.3 No other animals are allowed, except fish in a tank of 10 gallons or less and service or assistance animals as required by law.
6.4 Tenant is responsible for any damage, cleaning or pest treatment caused by the animal.

7. PARKING AND STORAGE
7.1 Tenant is assigned one parking space, number 14, in the rear lot. Additional spaces are not available.
7.2 Vehicles must be registered and in working order. Vehicles that do not run may be towed after 7 days' notice.
7.3 Each unit has one storage locker in the basement. Flammable materials may not be stored there.

8. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
8.1 Landlord will keep the building, the heating and plumbing systems and the appliances supplied with the Premises in good working order.
8.2 Tenant must report any problem needing repair through the resident portal within 48 hours of noticing it. A delay in reporting that makes the damage worse may make Tenant responsible for the added cost.
8.3 For emergencies such as flooding, no heat in winter, gas smell or a lockout, Tenant must call the 24-hour line at 555-0142 instead of using the portal.
8.4 Tenant will replace light bulbs and smoke-alarm batteries and keep drains free of grease and hair.
8.5 Except in an emergency, Landlord will give at least 24 hours' notice before entering the Premises for repairs or inspections.

9. ALTERATIONS
9.1 Tenant may hang pictures with small nails. Painting, wallpaper, new locks and any change to fixtures need Landlord's written consent.
9.2 Satellite dishes and window air-conditioning units may be installed only with written consent and by a licensed installer.

10. INSURANCE
10.1 Tenant must keep renter's insurance during the whole Term, with personal liability coverage of at least $100,000, and must give Landlord proof of coverage before move-in.
10.2 Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's belongings.

11. SUBLETTING AND ASSIGNMENT
11.1 Tenant may not sublet the Premises or assign this lease, in whole or in part, without Landlord's written consent.
11.2 Short-term rentals through any platform are not allowed under any circumstances.

12. ENDING THE LEASE
12.1 To move out at the end of the Term, Tenant must give written notice at least 60 days before October 31, 2027.
12.2 If Tenant does not give notice and Landlord does not offer a renewal, the tenancy continues month to month as described in section 1.3.
12.3 Tenant may end the lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two months' rent. The fee replaces any further rent owed after the move-out date.
12.4 On moving out, Tenant must return all keys, fobs and the parking permit, and leave the Premises clean and empty.

13. RULES AND NOTICES
13.1 Tenant agrees to follow the building rules posted in the lobby and on the resident portal, which Landlord may update with 30 days' notice.
13.2 Notices to Landlord must be sent through the resident portal or by mail to the management office. Notices to Tenant may be sent through the portal, by email or by mail to the Premises.

14. GENERAL
14.1 If any part of this lease is found unenforceable, the rest of the lease remains in effect.
14.2 This lease is the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant. Changes must be in writing and signed by both.
