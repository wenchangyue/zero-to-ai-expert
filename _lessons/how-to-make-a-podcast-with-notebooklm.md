## The problem

NotebookLM can turn your notes into a two-host podcast in a few minutes. But how long is it, and are the hosts saying what your notes say?

## The test

One page of made-up BIO 101 notes on photosynthesis (230 words), with two exam tips and a line saying C4 and CAM plants are not on the exam. Three new notebooks on 2026-11-17, each with the notes as the only source. In each notebook: the default Audio Overview, then Deep Dive with typed instructions, then Brief with the same instructions. Every episode was downloaded, transcribed and read against an answer key fixed before the first episode (the Brief arm was added after seeing the lengths; it is dated in the answer key).

| | Default (Deep Dive) | Deep Dive + instructions | Brief + instructions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 21:19, 21:21, 22:03 | 20:23, 20:32, 18:51 | 1:48, 1:36, 1:51 |
| Key points (of 10) | 10, 10, 10 | 10, 10, 10 | 10, 7, 10 (topics mentioned) |
| Things not in the notes (my count) | 27, 28, 26 | 6, 11, 13 | 0, 0, 0 |
| Invented exam date | 3 of 3 | 1 of 3 | 0 of 3 |
| Quiz at the end | — | 3 of 3, answers right | 3 of 3 (answers given in 2) |

The default hosts said the exam was "tomorrow", "Tuesday" and "next week". One said "the notes mentioned electron excitement"; they don't. With instructions, one host said "No extra fluff. No outside facts, just the notes." and the episode still added eleven. Counts depend on the counting unit (one row per distinct claim) by a few either way.

## Format, Focus, Check

1. **Format** (must). Pick Brief when you're reviewing; keep Deep Dive for when you want the long story.
2. **Focus** (important). In "What should the AI hosts focus on?", tell the hosts to use only your notes, name what they must say, and ask for a quiz at the end.
3. **Check** (must). Before you listen, write down the points you need. Tick each one as you hear it and read any you didn't tick in your notes. If the hosts say something you'd write on an exam, find it in your notes first.

## Boundary

One page of notes, three runs of each setting, one morning, a paid plan, English only. Interactive mode, the Short and Long length settings and other languages were not tested. Longer notes were not tested.
