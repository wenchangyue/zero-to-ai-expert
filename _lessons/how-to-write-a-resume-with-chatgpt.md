## The problem

You paste your resume and a job post into ChatGPT and ask for a tailored version. It reads better. But does every line still describe something you did?

## The test

One made-up resume (a coffee shop shift supervisor who runs the store's Instagram; dates, but no measured results) and one made-up marketing coordinator job post. Logged-out ChatGPT, a new chat for each run, three runs per prompt, on 2026-11-10. What counts as an addition (a new number, tool, title, degree or task) was fixed before the first run; judgment calls are listed as borderline and not counted.

| | Plain prompt | Rules prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear additions in the rewritten resume | 1, 2 and 6 | 0, 0 and 0 |
| Tools, job titles or degrees added | none | none |
| Borderline stretches (not counted) | 2 to 3 per chat | 1, 1 and 2 |
| Requirements the resume doesn't show | partly mentioned | all five listed, 3 of 3 |
| [ADD NUMBER] blanks | — | 2, 3 and 1 |

The plain-prompt additions included "social media graphics", "promotional signs", "reporting fundamentals", "content writing" and "2+ years of experience managing social media content". The resume never says how long she has run the account; "2+ years" is the job post's requirement. With the rules, one chat wrote "[ADD NUMBER] years of experience managing the store's Instagram account" instead.

## Facts, Blanks, Gaps

1. **Facts** (must). Tell it to use only what's in your resume, and name what it may not add: numbers, tools, titles, degrees.
2. **Blanks** (important). Ask for [ADD NUMBER] wherever a number would help, then fill it with a real one. No real number, no number.
3. **Gaps** (nice to have). Ask for the job's requirements your resume doesn't show: add them if you've done them, learn them if you haven't, or address them in a cover letter.

Then check: put the original next to the new version, find the source of every new line, look twice at words that also appear in the job post, and cut anything you couldn't talk about for a minute.

## Boundary

One made-up resume and one job post, three runs of each prompt, one morning, ChatGPT logged out. The rules removed the clear additions here, but small stretches still got through. How hiring software scores these resumes was not tested.
