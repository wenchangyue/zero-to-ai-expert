## The problem

You paste a long document into ChatGPT and ask for a summary. How do you get one that is short, and how do you know what it left out or got wrong?

## The test

One fictional apartment lease (14 sections, 1,088 words), ChatGPT on the web without logging in, three fresh chats per request, 2026-10-20 between 23:13 and 23:22 New York time. Each answer was checked against 12 must-know items for a tenant (correct number and condition). The third request was written after seeing the first two results.

| Request | Words | Must-know items covered | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "Summarize this lease." | 1,013–1,060 | 12 of 12, every run | about as long as the lease |
| Tenant: every amount, deadline and risky rule, with section numbers | 2,368–2,670 | 12 of 12, every run | sourced, more than twice as long |
| Tenant, under 200 words, with section numbers | 192 / 235 / 213 | 9 / 8 / 10 | all three left out the deposit-return deadline and did not clearly state the lease term; one stated the subletting rule wrongly |

A labeled ad appeared under 5 of the 10 answers. It is not part of the answer.

## Shape, Source, Spot-check

1. **Shape** (must). Say who you are, what you need from the document, and how long the answer should be. With a length, it will leave things out, so name the items that must stay.
2. **Source** (important). Ask for the section or page after each item.
3. **Spot-check** (must). Compare the summary with your own short list, ask directly for anything missing, and open the original for every line you'll rely on. Check the number and the condition around it.

## The check, done once

- The summary says "no subletting". Searching the lease for "sublet" finds the section heading, then §11.1: the tenant may not sublet "without Landlord's written consent". The rule is "get written consent first", not "never".
- The deposit deadline. Asked "What does the lease say about returning the deposit?", ChatGPT gave a deadline with a section number. Searching the lease for "return the deposit" finds §3.3: within 30 days after the tenant moves out and returns all keys. It matches.

## Boundary

One short fictional lease, ChatGPT without logging in, three runs per request, one night. Longer documents and uploaded files can behave differently. A summary is not legal advice: for a real lease, the original text and local law are what count.
